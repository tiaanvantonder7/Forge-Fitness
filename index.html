Backup and privacy

This first version stores data locally in this browser. It is not synced to a cloud account and isn't automatically shared with a coach. Clearing browser data may erase it. Export a backup regularly.

Smartwatch connection

Not connected in version 1. The app currently accepts steps, sleep and other readings manually. A later Android build can connect through Health Connect, subject to device permissions and supported watch data.

AI coaching

The coaching summary in this version uses simple local rules; it does not send your health data to an AI service. A real AI coach can be added later with your explicit consent and a secure service connection.

Reset app

This permanently clears data stored by this app in this browser. Export a backup first.
